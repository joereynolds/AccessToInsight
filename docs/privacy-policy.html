Privacy Policy

Access to Insight  ·  Last updated: September 2026

Short version: The app collects no personal data. Your reading history, bookmarks, and collections are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Access to Insight does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no registration of any kind.

Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device only:

  • Reading history (which texts you've opened and how far you've read)
  • Bookmarks and collections you create
  • Display preferences (font size, theme, line height)

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server controlled by us. It can be removed at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting services, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Internet access

The app does not make any network requests. All 1,800+ texts are bundled with the app and available fully offline.

Third parties

No data is shared with any third party because no data is collected in the first place.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date at the top.
